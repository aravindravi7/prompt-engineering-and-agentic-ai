## Description (HTML decoded, tags stripped)

Stripe is building the economic infrastructure for the internet. Millions of businesses use Stripe to accept payments, manage revenue, and grow globally. Stripe Tax helps businesses calculate, collect, and manage indirect taxes as they sell across products, markets, and business models.

We’re hiring a Staff Product Manager to help shape the future of Stripe Tax. In this role, you will lead high-impact product strategy and execution across a broad portfolio, making it easier for businesses to understand, calculate, collect, and manage tax as they grow.

What you’ll do

As a Staff Product Manager on Stripe Tax, you will identify the most important opportunities to improve our tax products and turn them into focused strategies, durable platforms, and exceptional user experiences.

You will work closely with Engineering, Design, Tax, Partnerships, Go-to-Market, and other product teams to:

Set strategy and lead product initiatives that help more businesses successfully adopt and use Stripe Tax.

Improve the speed, accuracy, reliability, and usability of Stripe Tax calculations and related workflows.

Expand support for the geographies, tax requirements, industries, products, and business models that matter most to Stripe users.

Build capabilities for complex tax needs, such as registrations, exemptions, address and fulfillment logic, tax customization, and other business-specific requirements.

Develop scalable products and platform capabilities that work across Stripe’s ecosystem, including integrations, connector apps, marketplaces, and Connect platforms.

Partner across Stripe to create coherent end-to-end experiences spanning calculation, collection, reporting, and tax management.

Use customer insight, market context, data, and technical understanding to prioritize investments and make clear tradeoffs.

Align senior stakeholders and cross-functional partners around a shared product direction, measurable outcomes, and an execution plan.

What success looks like

You will help make Stripe Tax a product businesses can rely on as they scale. Merchants should be able to confidently answer “yes” to questions such as:

Can I use Stripe Tax for my business and the locations where I sell?

Does Stripe Tax support the taxes and business models that apply to me?

Can it calculate tax accurately for my registrations, exemptions, customers, products, and fulfillment model?

Is it fast, reliable, and easy for my team to operate?

Does it work with the other Stripe products, platforms, and integrations my business depends on?

Tax is a fundamental challenge for businesses that sell across borders, channels, and business models. Stripe Tax has the opportunity to remove that complexity, helping businesses launch faster, expand with confidence, and operate globally.

This is a high-leverage role at the intersection of global commerce, financial infrastructure, and product platform strategy. You will shape how Stripe Tax evolves to serve the next generation of internet businesses.
