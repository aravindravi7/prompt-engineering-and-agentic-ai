About the team

The Foreign Exchange team builds the products and infrastructure that help Stripe users operate across currencies. Businesses increasingly operate globally from the moment they launch, collecting revenue, holding balances, paying suppliers, and managing expenses across multiple currencies.

Managing those currencies remains fragmented. Finance teams must decide what to hold, when to convert, how to manage currency exposure, and how to reconcile the results. Today, these workflows often span banks, spreadsheets, treasury systems, and specialist FX providers.

Stripe is uniquely positioned to simplify this. We sit at the center of the payments, Billing, balances, payouts, and settlement flows that create a business’s currency needs. Our vision is to make Stripe the financial home for businesses operating internationally, with a unified offering for holding, converting, and managing currencies and FX risk.

A core part of this vision is making currency management agentic. We believe software agents will increasingly help businesses understand their FX position, recommend actions, and manage currencies within defined policies and controls.

This could include Stripe-native agents that monitor exposure and take approved actions. It could also mean enabling customers’ own agents to use Stripe’s infrastructure to understand balances and exposures, obtain pricing, execute conversions or hedges, and reconcile the results.

Our goal is to make Stripe the currency-management layer that both people and agents can build on.

We will build these capabilities both for globally operating businesses and for the platforms and marketplaces that use Connect.

What you’ll do

We’re looking for a Product Manager to build a new area of Stripe’s FX and currency-management offering. This is a zero-to-one product role at the intersection of FX, treasury, and agentic AI. You will help define what agentic currency management should look like. That could mean Stripe-native agents that understand exposure, recommend actions, and execute within customer-defined policies. It could also mean enabling customers’ own agents to use Stripe’s FX infrastructure to understand balances, obtain pricing, manage risk, execute transactions, and reconcile the results.

You will work directly with globally operating businesses to understand their most important currency-management problems. You will identify the right initial product wedge and take it from discovery through launch and iteration. Today, treasury teams assemble exposures from multiple systems, determine hedge requirements, monitor changing positions, request pricing, execute trades, and reconcile the results. We believe much of this workflow can become continuous, intelligent, and increasingly automated.

You might build products that combine Stripe’s real-time view of payments, balances, and settlement activity with customer forecasts and policies to identify exposures and recommend actions. You might also define the APIs, permissions, controls, and financial primitives that allow customer or third-party agents to safely take those actions through Stripe.

Over time, these systems could move from surfacing insights to requesting quotes, executing conversions or hedges, rebalancing positions, and escalating exceptions within clearly defined permissions.

You will determine which intelligence Stripe should provide directly, which capabilities we should expose for others to build on, where humans should remain in the decision loop, and how we evaluate whether agents are making good decisions. Your product area could also include automated conversion and balance policies, recurring currency-management workflows, or capabilities distributed through Connect.

The opportunity is broad. Your job will be to make it focused. You will determine where Stripe can create differentiated value, build the first product, measure whether it works, and use those learnings to shape the roadmap.

You will work closely with Engineering and Design on product experiences, APIs, data models, agent behavior, controls, and system reliability. You will also partner with Treasury, Risk, Legal, Finance, Sales, and Product Marketing to ensure the products are economically sound, operationally scalable, and ready for customers.
