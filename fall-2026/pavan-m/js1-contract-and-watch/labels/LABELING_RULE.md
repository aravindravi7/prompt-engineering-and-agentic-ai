# Labeling rule — written October 30, 2026, 1:10 PM ET, before labeling any posting

I label a posting RELEVANT for Alex Morgan when it involves backend software engineering, Java/Spring Boot, distributed systems, APIs, cloud infrastructure, platform engineering, or data engineering, and the role reasonably fits his experience with Kafka, cloud technologies, and production systems. I also consider general Software Engineer roles relevant, but primarily frontend, mobile, or machine learning research roles are NOT RELEVANT unless they have substantial backend or infrastructure responsibilities. Senior and mid-level roles can be relevant, but Staff and Principal positions are generally NOT RELEVANT because they typically require broader technical leadership and substantially more experience. Alex prefers US-based opportunities and is open to remote roles, but postings must offer a workable US location or remote arrangement. For borderline cases, I lean toward NOT RELEVANT unless the posting provides clear evidence of a strong match with Alex's skills and experience.

## How this differs from the contract rule (intentional)
- Wider roles: platform, infrastructure, and data engineering can be relevant here; the contract's title check accepts only titles containing Alex's target_titles.
- Staff and Principal roles are excluded here; the contract allows them.
- Any workable US location or US-remote counts here; the contract accepts only Mountain View, San Francisco, or any location containing "Remote" (including non-US remote).

## Limits of these labels
These labels are one person's judgment, not ground truth. "Reasonably fits" and "clear evidence" are judgment calls. Agreement with my labels measures agreement with me, not correctness.

## Selection method
From the 130 postings in evidence/2026-10-30_manual-capture_gleanwork.json, sorted by title, I picked 14 technical and 6 non-technical postings by title only, before reading any description, avoiding duplicate titles. The dev/held-out split is random with a fixed seed (7375), done before labeling.
